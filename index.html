Content wins at scale when the operation behind it is engineered. These are the systems and programmes I designed, built and run.

Featured project · Full-stack platform

Content SEO Operations Platform

3,700+ articles across 19 markets, managed in one place instead of spreadsheets

Designed and built, using Claude Code and Codex as coding agents, the platform that runs Vantage Academy. It turns Search Console data into a weekly worklist (refresh, quick win, prune, content gap) and drafts optimisations through an AI skill pipeline with fact-checking rules built in.

How it works
  • A WordPress-synced inventory of every Academy article, with Search Console analytics from BigQuery.
  • Weekly opportunity scoring and traffic-drop alerts feed the worklist.
  • An internal-linking engine audits 23,000+ links and suggests new ones from each page's own search queries.
  • Nothing goes live on its own: once a draft clears manual review and, where required, Compliance sign-off, one click publishes it to WordPress. Every change is confirmed on the live page and can be undone.
  • Backed by 2,300+ automated tests and checks that trace every figure on screen to its source.
