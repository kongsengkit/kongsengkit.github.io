Content SEO Operations Platform

Academy impact: clicks roughly doubled in five months · 3,300+ queries on page one · the brand's highest-traffic content clusters

Designed and built the platform that runs Vantage Academy: 3,700+ articles across 19 markets, managed in one place instead of spreadsheets. It turns Search Console data into a weekly worklist (refresh, quick win, prune, content gap) and drafts optimisations through an AI skill pipeline with fact-checking rules built in.

Nothing goes live on its own: once a draft clears manual review and, where required, Compliance sign-off, one click publishes it to WordPress in place of manual posting.
